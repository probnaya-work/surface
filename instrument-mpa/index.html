MPA–01

PROB–MPA–01 · MACHINE PORTRAIT APPARATUS

The apparatus reads one photograph of a human subject on a 16 × 16 grid, ranks the 256 readings into five levels, and draws the record as a mark. The output is a machine portrait: a deterministic derivative of a photograph, not a picture of a person.
