MPA–01 PROB–MPA–01 · MACHINE PORTRAIT APPARATUS

The apparatus reads one photograph of a human subject on a 32 × 32 grid, cuts the 1024 readings into five fixed tonal bands, and draws each band as a mark. The output is a machine portrait: a deterministic derivative of a photograph, not a picture of a person.

Two properties are set by the operator: which derivation reads the specimen, and which field it is drawn on. Nothing else is adjustable. The photograph is what is derived, not the person, so a second photograph of the same subject will not return the same portrait.

The bands are cut on the plate's own clipped range, so the ink budget is not equalised. A subject against a bright ground spends less ink than the same subject against a dark one, and the two plates diverge accordingly.
