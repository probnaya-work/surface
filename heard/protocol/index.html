PROTOTYPE STATUS
18 SEP 2026
CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION / SYNTHETIC ONLY

HEARD currently runs entirely from invented examples in the browser. It collects no public posts, identifies no authors, sends no material to a model, and stores no observations. The rules below describe requirements for a future live system, not behavior already in operation.

01 / SCOPE

What HEARD would observe

HEARD is intended to notice how people describe their relationships with increasingly capable machines in selected public spaces. A future source must be named, its rules and access terms checked, and its inclusion disclosed before collection starts. Private spaces, direct messages, and submitted personal accounts are outside this protocol.

Any field view describes only the chosen sources and time window. It cannot stand for what “people” or society as a whole think.

02 / RECORD

What a public record contains

A retained expression would carry a short meaning-preserving paraphrase, theme labels, broad context, observed time, and the reading to which it contributes. It would carry no username, profile, direct URL, exact quotation, location, or identifying biographical detail.

Paraphrasing alone is not a guarantee of anonymity. Distinctive experiences may still be traceable, so a record must also pass a reidentification review before publication.

03 / PROCESSING

From expression to signal

  1. Check the source's permission and the expression's relevance.
  2. Reject sensitive or identifying material before a public record is made.
  3. Paraphrase the meaning without copying distinctive wording; assign themes and broad context.
  4. Review for reidentification, duplication, distortion, and misleading context.
  5. Publish only retained records and aggregate readings with the source set and time window disclosed.

No automated classifier or editorial workflow has been connected yet. These are gates to design and test before any live collection.

04 / EXCLUSIONS

What stays out

Exclude material about health, mental health, sexuality, politics, religion, children, crises, or other sensitive personal circumstances; identifying or unusually specific stories; private or access-restricted content; harassment; and material whose meaning depends on unavailable context. Reject uncertain cases instead of forcing them into a theme.

05 / RETENTION & REMOVAL

Keep less; correct quickly

A live version would retain raw text and source identifiers only for the shortest processing window needed for review, then delete them. A concrete duration, deletion mechanism, and audit procedure must be published before live collection begins. The present prototype retains none of these because it has no input pipeline.

Published paraphrases and readings must be removable. Requests about misrepresentation, identification, or removal can be sent to mail@probnaya.work. A future live system must define how it can locate and remove a record without keeping a public link to its source.

06 / READINGS & LIMITS

Interpretation remains visible

A tremor marks an unusual connection or concentration in the observed sample; a drift marks a slower change. An issued reading separates the material observed from the interpretation offered by PROBNAYA, names the source set and interval, and records uncertainty and protocol version.

HEARD must not present its themes as diagnoses, counts as population statistics, or movement as a claim of causation. The demo reading is an example of editorial form, not evidence of a real trend.
