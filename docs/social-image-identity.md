# PROBNAYA social-image identity

## Approved decision

The FIELDWORK identity review established this requirement: keep a project's
creative idea, but its Open Graph and Twitter/X images must inherit the style
and typography of the existing PROBNAYA social images. Sharing only whitespace,
large text or a restrained palette is insufficient.

This is a social-image rule. It does not require changing a project's own page
typography or making every social image use the same layout.

## Reference assets and source

- `assets/og-1200x630.png`: the laboratory's main identity.
- `assets/og-objects-1200x630.png`: an independent composition within that identity.
- `assets/og-observations-1200x630.png`: the brand mark, typography and editorial hierarchy.
- `scripts/og/observations-og.html`: executable reference for Archivo, Geist Mono,
  the PROBNAYA mark and wordmark, spacing and 1200 × 630 output.

Inspect these images before creating or revising a social preview. The images
show permitted variation; the source establishes the actual typefaces and mark.

## What carries across projects

- Archivo for the principal title, large notation and PROBNAYA wordmark.
- Geist Mono for labels, references and supporting notation.
- The existing PROBNAYA mark and wordmark where a brand signature is needed;
  do not approximate it using the project's own font or invent a new lockup.
- Deliberate scale, generous space, precise alignment and restrained rules.
- A clearly readable title and relationship to PROBNAYA at social-preview size.

Project-specific color, imagery and composition may vary intentionally. Do not
automatically add rulers, diagrams or blue accents merely to imitate the main
image. Do not derive a new OG identity from the project's website alone.

## FIELDWORK application

Keep its warm paper, ink, moss and earth palette, oversized geographical
notation, and spatial composition. The project image may include a restrained
photographic accent. Use Archivo and Geist Mono in the social images even though
the FIELDWORK page uses Plex and Newsreader. Distinguish FIELDWORK, the practice,
from TRÁS-OS-MONTES, its first project.

The FIELDWORK repository owns its reproducible generator, assets and font
licenses. Its practice image does not imply a published practice page.

## Review

Compare both full-size and reduced images with the references above. Verify
1200 × 630 output, legibility, no clipped labels, and accurate factual notation.
Keep fonts and their licenses available locally for reproducible generation.
Any departure from this typographic relationship needs an explicit editorial
decision rather than an inferred project exception.
